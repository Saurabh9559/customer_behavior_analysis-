# customer_behavior_analysis-
## 🎯 Business Impact Snapshot

This project simulates a real-world retail analytics scenario where stakeholders seek to understand evolving customer purchasing behavior across demographics, product categories, and engagement channels.

### 📌 Problem Context

Retail leadership observed shifts in purchasing patterns and required a consolidated analytical view to identify:

* Key revenue-driving customer segments
* Impact of discounts and subscriptions on spending
* Product category performance trends
* Behavioral indicators of customer loyalty

### 📊 Analytical Scale

* **3,900+ transactions analyzed**
* **18 behavioral and demographic attributes processed**
* **10+ business-driven SQL analyses executed**
* **Interactive Power BI dashboard with KPI tracking and segmentation views**

### 💼 Business Value Delivered

✔ Identified high-value customer segments for targeted marketing
✔ Highlighted product categories suitable for promotional prioritization
✔ Demonstrated subscription influence on loyalty and revenue contribution
✔ Provided data-backed recommendations for discount strategy optimization

This project demonstrates practical capability in transforming raw transactional data into actionable insights, supporting strategic decision-making through analytics and visualization.
